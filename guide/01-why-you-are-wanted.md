# 1. Why you are wanted

The Drupal AI Initiative builds the `drupal/ai` module ecosystem: providers, agents, chatbots, CKEditor tools, translation, content review, the Context Control Center (CCC), and the public [AI demo](https://new.drupal.org/ai/demo). Most contributors are developers. A small UX track reviews what they ship and is short on hands.

Three lanes, all with design work:

- **Inside AI**: AI features in Drupal's admin UI. Chat panels, review sidebars, settings forms, menu placement. Most UX requests land here.
- **Outside AI**: external agents (Claude Code, Cursor) acting on Drupal through MCP and the Tool API. Little UI; naming and documentation work.
- **People**: how site builders, editors and reviewers see what the AI did and why. A discovery and information-architecture problem.

Recent UX-track output: the Rotterdam demo site, story-by-story usability reviews of the demo with Figma decks, low-fidelity explorations of the Ask AI chat controls, an AI admin menu hierarchy now used across modules.

What is missing and what you bring: cold-read usability testing, content design for labels and help text, accessibility review, and sketches that turn a list of problems into a proposal. The track's rule: every review pairs with low-fidelity exploration so reviews produce solutions, not only problems.

The official page, [How to Contribute to UX](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai/1.4.x/ux/how_to_contribute/), asks for UX plus Drupal experience and ongoing involvement. Read it as a preference. Story reviews are being done by people new to Drupal; the demo exists for them.
