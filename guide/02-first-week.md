# 2. First week checklist

Do these in order. Each takes minutes except the meeting.

1. **Accounts.** Create a [drupal.org](https://www.drupal.org/user/register) account. Sign in to [git.drupalcode.org](https://git.drupalcode.org) with it (button "Sign in with Drupal.org"). All issues live on GitLab now.
2. **Slack.** Join [Drupal Slack](https://www.drupal.org/slack), then the channels `#ai-ux` (the UX track), `#ai` (general), `#ai-contrib` (sprint coordination).
3. **Introduce yourself in `#ai-ux`** using [templates/intro-message.md](../templates/intro-message.md). Say which skills you offer and that you can take a story review. Ask for a buddy for your first review; pairing is normal. A product owner will match you.
4. **Start a demo instance** at https://new.drupal.org/ai/demo (Drupal Forge, free, 30 days). Log in as the editor it gives you. Open the **AI Features** button and read one story.
5. **Read the five UX docs pages**, 20 minutes total: [How to Contribute](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai/1.4.x/ux/how_to_contribute/), [Core UX Principles](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai/1.4.x/ux/core_ux_principles/), [Organizing AI Modules & Features](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai/1.4.x/ux/organizing_ai_modules_and_features/), [Request UX Support](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai/1.4.x/ux/request_ux_support/), [UX Improvement Projects](https://project.pages.drupalcode.org/ai/1.4.x/ux/ux_improvement_projects/).
6. **Read the current UX meeting issue.** Weekly, Mondays. One issue per meeting in the `ai_initiative` project, titled `Drupal AI UX meeting <date>`; notes, decisions and owners are in the description. Find this week's: https://git.drupalcode.org/project/ai_initiative/-/issues/?search=UX%20meeting&state=opened. Ask for the call time in `#ai-ux`; it is not published.
7. **Run the radar in newcomer mode** ([README](../README.md); `--starter`, or tell the assistant you are new). It opens with Good first picks and links a finished story review to read first. Read that review and its follow-up issues before claiming anything; it is the shape your own write-up takes. First pick for most people: an unclaimed story review ([04-story-review.md](04-story-review.md)).
8. **Claim it.** Comment on the issue with what you will do and when ("ETD Friday"), assign yourself (the sidebar; if you cannot, say so in the comment and someone will assign you). Post your findings before the next Monday meeting.

Figma: the track keeps a shared board; ask for the link in `#ai-ux`. Per-review decks are linked from the meeting issues.
