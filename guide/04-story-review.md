# 4. How to do a story review

A story is a demo page explaining one AI feature and walking the visitor through trying it. A story review tests whether the story and the feature work for someone who has never seen Drupal. Reference issue: [UX review: AI Translate story](https://git.drupalcode.org/project/ai_demo/-/issues/3628683). A finished one to read first: [UX review: Ask AI story](https://git.drupalcode.org/project/ai_demo/-/issues/3628675) with its follow-ups, the [UI changes issue](https://git.drupalcode.org/project/ai_demo/-/issues/3628686) and the [story fixes issue](https://git.drupalcode.org/project/ai_demo/-/issues/3628687). The radar's newcomer mode links the most recent finished review. The `ai-ux-story-review` skill builds the protocol and board from the issue, and later drafts the write-up; this page is the human summary.

1. **Set up.** Fresh demo instance, logged out. Board in Figma, Miro or Google Docs with comments open ([templates/story-review-board.md](../templates/story-review-board.md)). Comment the board link on the issue; assign yourself.
2. **Task 1, read cold.** Story page only. Is it clear what the feature does and why an editor cares? Which words are unclear? What problem does it solve for you, how do you solve it today, who else does this?
3. **Task 2, follow the story.** Story and demo side by side; log in when told. Screenshot every step. Where did you not know what to do? Did instructions stay visible? Did the result match the screenshots? Which labels or dialogs were unclear?
4. **Task 3, the feature.** The issue's own questions (for Translate: can an editor trust and review the output). Say what you could not judge.
5. **Task 4, sketch.** For the top two or three problems, an annotated crop or sketch with the one-line change. This is what makes the review actionable.
6. **Post.** Severity 1–4 per finding. Summary comment on the review issue (board link, three most important problems, story recommendations). One `ai_demo` issue bundling UI changes as a table of number, change, severity, slide; one for story-text fixes, marked blocked by the UI issue; one per demo bug. Formats are in the `ai-ux-story-review` skill. Bring the deck to the next Monday meeting.
