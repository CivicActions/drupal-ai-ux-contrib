# 7. Etiquette and policy

- **AI disclosure is mandatory** for any significant AI-written text or design artefact you post. [Policy](https://www.drupal.org/docs/develop/issues/issue-procedures-and-etiquette/policy-on-the-use-of-ai-when-contributing-to-drupal). The initiative's issue templates carry a three-checkbox block; copy it into anything you post:

  ```
  AI disclosure: how this was written
  - [ ] Verified by a person: I reviewed the whole text and confirmed every step, result and claim myself.
  - [ ] Reviewed by a person: I read it and believe it is accurate, but did not verify every detail.
  - [ ] AI-generated: an AI agent wrote this, and no person has reviewed it yet.
  ```

  Posting unreviewed AI output in comments or reviews is listed as an etiquette violation. The radar and the other skills in this kit draft; you read, edit, tick the box, and post.
- **You post, not the tool.** Nothing in this kit writes to GitLab or Slack.
- **Answer feedback.** An unanswered question on your issue, or a `state::needsWork` you leave hanging, reads as abandonment and the work is reassigned.
- **Claim before you start**, with a date. Unclaim with a one-line note if you cannot finish.
- **Credit** is recorded automatically on drupal.org when you comment or are assigned; set a default attribution (your employer) once in your drupal.org profile.
- **Tone.** Findings are about the screen, not the author. Lead with what the user could not do.
