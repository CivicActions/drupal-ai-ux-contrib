# Story review board outline

Create in Figma, Miro or Google Docs; enable comments for anyone with the link; paste the link on the review issue before you start.

```
Title: UX review — <story name> — <your name>, <date>
Reviewer context: Drupal experience <none/some/much>; <language or domain notes the issue asks for>
Instance: Drupal Forge, started <date>

1. Story read-through (logged out)
   - What I think the feature does, in one sentence
   - Why an editor would care, in one sentence
   - Unclear words or sentences (quote them)
   - Problem this solves for me / how I solve it today / other products that do this

2. Walkthrough (one frame per story step)
   - Step N: could I do it? what did I click? screenshot
   - Did the instructions stay visible?
   - Did the result match the story's screenshot?
   - Labels, screens, dialogs that were unclear (quote exact text)

3. Feature-specific questions from the issue
   - ...

4. Findings, ranked (one row each)
   | # | Severity (blocker / major / minor) | What happened | Where (path) | Who it affects | Evidence frame |

5. Low-fidelity exploration (top 2–3 findings)
   - Annotated crop or sketch per finding, with the one-line change it proposes

6. Open questions for the Monday meeting
```
